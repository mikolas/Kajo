01 // Etymology & Brand Architecture Finnish "kajo" = glimmer, dawn horizon light, refracted ray in darkness

1. The Metaphor ("Valonkajo")

A razor-sharp sliver of refracted cyan/blue light cutting through pitch-black obsidian glass. Never soft, never pastel, never rounded.

2. Zero-Radius Discipline (0px)

The current data/icons/kajo.svg uses an iOS squircle (rx="112"). A true Kajo emblem must use a strict 0px square or precision 45° chamfered chassis.

3. Dual-Scale Legibility

Must read unmistakably at both 512×512 (full Aero specular gradients) and 16×16 monochrome symbolic (panel launcher button & HUD).

4. Modular Suite Coherence

Establishes a shared structural chassis for kajo, kajo-ide, kajo-files, kajo-player, kajo-mod, kajo-settings, and kajo-greeter.
